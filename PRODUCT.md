# Langouste public course workbook

The public static course teaches beginner Hungarian and Egyptian Arabic through adapted AI-news readings and authored practice. Learners need readable passages, nearby language explanations and honest records of what they actually attempted. It is a study tool, not a marketing page or live news feed.

The course contains 30 days per language and dated supplements. Readings, vocabulary senses, phrases, examples, grammar, accepted answer variants, sources and stable learning IDs are authored content. Preserve the content files and semantic distinctions. Do not infer morphology beyond supplied forms or present source paraphrases as quotations.

The public site requires no account and stores progress in the browser under existing version-one lesson keys. Preserve previous attempts, dates, reading acknowledgements and completion. Export is a backup; there is no cross-device sync or import in this version. Account-connected Langouste and its existing review scheduler remain separate and unchanged.

Activity is not mastery. Matching, hints, translations, transliteration, supplied examples, revealed answers and self-assessment are supported practice. A first unassisted response is same-session evidence, not proven retention. Older records may lack support detail and must not be upgraded retroactively. The public review destination lists questions whose last answer was incorrect; it is explicitly not a spaced-review schedule.

Use compact textbook structure, readable body text, clear language/dialect labels, sentence-level meanings, separate transliteration and contextual reference. Preserve keyboard and mobile operation and Arabic block direction with mixed-language isolation. Audio buttons appear only for actual prerecorded assets; never fall back to synthetic browser speech.
